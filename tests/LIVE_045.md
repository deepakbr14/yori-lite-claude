## Costs and limits
- Substantive turns write more: chat 2's answer turn saved 9 rows plus a 1-row reconciliation (3 Sheets calls, being the chat's first save). Small-fact turns are unchanged.
- Timing for these turns was not measured.
- Interests arose only when prompted by the conversation; none was invented at setup.
- An existing Cortex keeps its old identity row; the new starting Self text applies to new setups.
