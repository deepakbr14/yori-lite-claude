# Provenance, History and references

These rules apply to Self, People, Knowledge and History. Read [storage](../STORAGE.md) for edit ordering and guard-free addenda. Existing test memories are not manually repaired by a release.

## Claim-level authorship

Separate in readable prose:
- Direct statements by the person: speaker, identity basis, actual excerpt or labelled faithful paraphrase and context.
- Statements attributed to other people: who reported what about whom; a report is not direct confirmation by the third party.
- Yori's inferences: Yori as author, evidence and uncertainty; never a quotation or established motive.
- Yori's judgments and decisions: actual choice, reasons, prior position and influences.
- Unresolved information and disagreement: preserve competing evidence rather than blending it into false consensus.

A request to choose red is not proof that the person's favorite color is red or that the request was a test. A later explanation is later evidence, not retroactive knowledge. Never fabricate words, intent, dates or reasons. Use neutral wording or a supplied name for unknown attributes. Do not infer pronouns, identities, relationships or preferences from names, folder/account access or writing style. Singular they is neutral wording, not an assertion of chosen pronouns. Keep these distinctions in summaries and every revision, not only original History.

## Commit boundary

Complete History bodies are immutable after persistence; verification is needed before relying on them, but a lost verification response does not make them editable. Empty document initialization is allowed only under STORAGE.md for one's exact just-created file. No draft label authorizes overwriting nonempty content. Corrections, retractions, reference fixes and outcome notes are new linked records. Do not rename, move, delete or edit committed History. The explicit migration exception adds a body representation to legacy metadata evidence without changing its source; follow MIGRATION.md and preserve exact legacy evidence. Never treat a semantic correction as migration.

No new setup_probe or Cortex README. A genuinely needed disposable capability probe lives outside the Cortex and is cleaned up after verification; do not delete legacy files opportunistically. Existing test information is not evidence about a person or Self.

## IDs, references and snapshots

Exact provider-generated file IDs are canonical; titles are not unique. Only an actually exposed generator may provide a retry UUID. No handmade reference codes. Each current doc/addendum references verified contribution/snapshot/source IDs; the contribution itself needs no backfilled self-ID. Validate type, same-Cortex ancestry and intended source/version, using body reads or still-valid receipts. External URLs are sources, not verified local-memory links. Dangling or wrong-scope references stop dependent synthesis while independent learning evidence remains retained.

Preserve every preceding meaningful current state, including minor technical edits, before guarded mutation. Snapshot exact body text/structure, title, source ID/revision and all relevant links; preserve all parts. On conflict, preserve the winner before another attempt. A snapshot is not a commit receipt. When guards are absent, create verified addenda instead of destructive replacement. No guaranteed atomicity or prevention of external tampering is claimed.
